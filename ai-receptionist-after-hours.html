Best AI Receptionist for After-Hours Calls

After-hours answering can be one of the clearest reasons for a small business to use an AI receptionist—but only if the routing rules match the business.

What to compare

  • 24/7 availability and after-hours schedules.
  • Whether calls can be forwarded from your existing number.
  • Human escalation when an urgent issue needs a person.
  • Call summaries and recordings for next-day follow-up.
  • Spam filtering and caller handling.
Do not judge an after-hours service solely by “24/7” on the feature list. The important question is what happens to a real caller at 10 p.m.
