AI receptionists offer another option: software that can answer calls, handle common questions, schedule appointments and route important callers while you focus on running the business.

What Is an AI Receptionist?

An AI receptionist is software that answers business phone calls using conversational artificial intelligence.

Depending on the system, it can greet callers, answer frequently asked questions, collect lead information, schedule appointments, transfer calls to employees and handle calls outside normal business hours.

The important distinction is that an AI receptionist is not simply voicemail. Its purpose is to interact with the caller and perform useful actions during the conversation.

What Should a Small Business Look For?

1. Reliable Call Answering

The basic job is simple: answer the phone consistently. Look for 24/7 availability, customizable greetings and the ability to handle calls when employees are busy.

2. Appointment Booking

Appointment booking can make an AI receptionist much more useful. Instead of simply taking a message, the system can check availability and book a suitable appointment.

3. After-Hours Calls

Many small businesses receive calls after closing time. An AI receptionist can continue answering those calls rather than sending every customer directly to voicemail.

4. Human Call Transfer

Automation should not mean that every caller is trapped in automation. For important or complicated calls, look for a system that can transfer the caller to a real team member.

5. Integrations

Calendar and CRM integrations can turn a phone conversation into an actual business workflow. The best system is therefore not necessarily the one with the most AI features, but the one that fits the tools a business already uses.

6. Pricing Model

AI receptionists use different billing models. Some charge per call, some use credits or usage, and others combine a platform subscription with AI usage.

Are AI Receptionists Worth It for a Small Business?

They can be — particularly when phone calls are an important source of customers and the business regularly misses calls because nobody is available to answer.

The value isn't simply replacing a receptionist. It's about making sure the business has a consistent first response to incoming calls without requiring an employee to stay beside the phone.

A good test is to calculate how many calls your business currently misses and how many of those calls could reasonably become customers. If the number is meaningful, even a relatively inexpensive AI receptionist can become an operational tool rather than just another software subscription.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an AI receptionist cost?

Pricing varies significantly. In this comparison, Upfirst starts at $24.95/month, Sona has usage-based tiers beginning at $0 for 1,000 credits, and Nextiva XBert starts at $99/month for 100 AI interactions.

Can an AI receptionist book appointments?

Yes. Appointment booking is one of the main reasons businesses use AI receptionists. The exact calendars and scheduling tools supported vary by provider.

Can an AI receptionist answer calls after hours?

Yes. All three options reviewed here are designed to provide AI-powered call handling beyond normal business hours.

Can an AI receptionist transfer calls to a human?

Yes. Human handoff is available with the providers reviewed here, although the exact transfer workflow differs between platforms.

Can I keep my existing business phone number?

Depending on the provider and setup, yes. For example, Upfirst supports call forwarding from existing phones, including cell phones, landlines and VoIP systems.

Is an AI receptionist cheaper than a human receptionist?

An AI receptionist generally costs much less than hiring a dedicated full-time employee, but the two are not identical. Human receptionists can handle nuanced conversations and situations that require judgment beyond an automated workflow.
