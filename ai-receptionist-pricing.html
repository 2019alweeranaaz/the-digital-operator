AI Receptionist Pricing: What Small Businesses Should Compare

The headline monthly price is only the beginning. Compare the unit of usage, included allowance, overage cost and what is bundled with the service.

The four common pricing questions

  • Is billing based on calls, minutes, credits or AI interactions?
  • How much usage is included?
  • What happens when the allowance is exceeded?
  • Does the price include the underlying business phone system?
For example, a $25 plan with 30 calls and a $99 plan with 100 AI interactions are not directly comparable until you understand what counts as a call or interaction and what else is included.

How to estimate your real cost

Start with your average monthly inbound volume. Then separate routine calls, appointment requests, calls requiring a human and after-hours calls. The provider whose pricing best matches your actual workload may be cheaper even if its headline price is higher.
