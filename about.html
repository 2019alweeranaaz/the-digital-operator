About the publication

The practical side of AI for business.

The Digital Operator is an independent technology publication focused on practical AI tools, business software and systems that can make everyday operations more efficient.

What we cover

We look at AI and software through a business-operator lens: what a tool does, who it is for, what it costs, where it fits into a workflow and where its limitations matter.

Our editorial principle

“Less hype. More utility.”

We aim to explain software in plain language and separate useful capabilities from marketing noise. Product information can change, so readers should verify current pricing and terms before purchasing.

How we approach reviews

  • We prioritize practical business use cases.
  • We compare meaningful differences rather than feature-counting.
  • We identify important limitations and pricing conditions.
  • Where commercial relationships exist, we disclose them clearly.
