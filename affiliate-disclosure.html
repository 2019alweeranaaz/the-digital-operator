Commercial transparency

Affiliate Disclosure

The Digital Operator may use affiliate links in some articles. If a reader clicks an affiliate link and later makes a qualifying purchase or subscription, the publication may receive a commission at no additional cost to the reader.

What this means for our comparisons

Affiliate relationships do not change the need for an article to explain pricing, features, limitations and suitability. Products should be discussed according to their usefulness for the stated business use case—not simply according to commission.

How links will be identified

Where an article contains affiliate links, the commercial relationship will be disclosed clearly on the page. Affiliate links should only be added after the relevant program has actually approved the publication.

Prices and offers

Software pricing, trials, commissions and promotional offers can change. Readers should confirm the current terms on the provider's own website before purchasing.
