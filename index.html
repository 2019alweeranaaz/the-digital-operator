Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

What is The Digital Operator?

A practical publication focused on AI tools, automation and business software for modern operators.

How do you compare AI tools?

We focus on pricing, useful features, integrations, limitations and the business problem a tool solves.

Are AI receptionists worth it?

They can be useful for businesses that receive frequent calls, need appointment handling or want after-hours coverage.

Will you cover tools beyond AI receptionists?

Yes. The site is designed to expand into AI automation, productivity, customer service and other business software categories.
